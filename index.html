    Frequently Asked Questions

    What types of assets can I log? +

    Trader's Log supports a wide range of assets including stocks, options, futures, forex, cryptocurrencies, and commodities. You can customize your entries to fit any market.

    Is my trading data secure? +

    Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your data. All information is stored on secure cloud servers with regular backups.

    Can I import my historical trades? +

    Yes, our platform supports importing historical trade data via CSV files from most brokers. Our support team can assist you with the import process if needed.

    Do you offer a free trial? +

    Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for our Pro plan, no credit card required. This allows you to experience all the advanced features before committing.
